How it works

The clip fits onto an existing collar. The device then connects to the clip through the magnetic interface.

  1. 1

    Prepare the device

    Charge by USB-C, switch it on, and select a comfortable volume.

  2. →
  3. 2

    Fit the magnetic clip

    Slide the compact clip securely onto the dog’s existing collar.

  4. →
  5. 3

    Connect and go

    Align the device with the clip and allow the magnetic interface to connect.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

This is a development-stage demo. Final claims and specifications should be confirmed after product validation and compliance testing.

Is CalmClip a dog collar?

No. CalmClip is a compact electronic device designed to attach to an existing dog collar.

What sound does it produce?

The product concept uses continuous white noise intended to mask sudden environmental sounds.

How is the device controlled?

The current prototype includes volume up, power, and volume down buttons, plus visible status indicators.

Is it waterproof?

The current concept targets a water-resistant enclosure. A final rating should only be stated after formal testing.

Is this the final production product?

No. The imagery and website present a development prototype and design direction. Final production details may change.
